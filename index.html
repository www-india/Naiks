Our Specialities

Comprehensive Dental Excellence

A full spectrum of dental care — from preventive hygiene to complex full-arch implant rehabilitation — all under one premium roof.

🔩
Dental Implants

State-of-the-art titanium implants that look, feel and function like natural teeth. Dr. Bhargav specialises in single, multiple and full-arch implant restorations.

  • Single Tooth Implants
  • All-on-4 / All-on-6
  • Implant Supported Bridges
  • Immediate Loading Implants
🦷
Endodontics

Expert root canal treatments using the latest rotary systems and apex locators, ensuring maximum preservation of your natural tooth structure.

  • Root Canal Treatment (RCT)
  • Retreatment & Apicoectomy
  • Pulp Capping Procedures
  • Post & Core Restorations
✨
Aesthetic Dentistry

Transform your smile with our luxury cosmetic dentistry range — crafted to deliver natural beauty and long-lasting confidence.

  • Porcelain Veneers
  • Smile Designing
  • Teeth Whitening
  • Composite Bonding
👑
Prosthodontics

Comprehensive tooth replacement solutions including custom crowns, bridges and full-mouth rehabilitation to restore form and function.

  • Zirconia & Ceramic Crowns
  • Fixed Dental Bridges
  • Full Mouth Rehabilitation
  • Metal-Free Prosthetics
🛡️
Preventive Care

Evidence-based preventive dentistry to keep your smile healthy for life — including professional cleaning, sealants and dietary counselling.

  • Professional Scaling & Polishing
  • Dental Sealants
  • Fluoride Application
  • Periodontal Therapy

Oral Surgery & More

From simple extractions to complex oral surgical procedures, all performed with precise technique and patient comfort as our top priority.
